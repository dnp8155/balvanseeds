Balvan Agro Seeds | Hybrid & Improved Seeds Supplier Gujarat India

Balvan Agro Seeds is a leading supplier of certified hybrid & improved seeds in Gujarat, India. High-yield bajra, wheat, mustard & spices for farmers across 7 Indian states including Gujarat, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, and Madhya Pradesh.

Research-Driven Hybrid Seeds for High-Yield Indian Harvests

At Balvan Agro Seeds, our research-driven hybrid seeds and improved seed varieties empower Indian farmers to achieve resilient, high-yield harvests. From Kharif pearl millet (bajra) to Rabi wheat, mustard, cumin, and multi-cut fodder crops, every seed variety is rigorously developed for real field conditions.

Farmer-First Philosophy — Bij Balvan, Kishaan Dhanwan

Founded in 2014 by Shri Vajabhai Patel in Tharad, Banaskantha, Gujarat, Balvan Agro Seeds is built on a farmer-first conviction: "Balvan stands with me." A seed is no ordinary product—it carries the farmer's trust, hope, and the first foundation of his family's prosperous future.

Certified Seed Quality & Agricultural Testing

All Balvan Agro Seeds varieties undergo stringent germination, genetic purity, and vigor testing backed by ISO 9001:2015 quality standards and state seed certification agencies. Our certified seeds are processed in state-of-the-art facilities in Tharad, Gujarat.
