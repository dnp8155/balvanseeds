Balavan Agro Seeds — Hybrid & Improved Seeds Supplier, Gujarat India

Balavan Agro Seeds is an ISO 9001:2015 certified agricultural seed company based in Tharad, Dudhva, Gujarat, India. We supply research-driven hybrid and improved seed varieties for bajra (pearl millet), wheat, mustard, groundnut, cumin, fennel, isabgol, sesame, castor, moong (green gram), urad (black gram), soybean, sunflower, coriander, guar (cluster bean), sorghum (jowar), paddy (rice), chickpea (chana), maize, cotton, tomato, chilli, okra, watermelon, muskmelon, cowpea, fodder and vegetables to farmers across Gujarat, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Haryana, Punjab and Uttar Pradesh.
